## Reading guide (one paragraph)
The three experiments do **not** share a single testbed, so the only fully
cross-consistent column is **1−ᾱ (realized saturation)** — gain-independent and
defined identically for P1.1 and P1.2 (both at the canonical PD gains
kp=5e-5, kd=1e-3). Read 1−ᾱ as the portability axis: it falls monotonically
from ~1.0 (magnetic-only 3MTQ+0RW, fully torque-starved) through ~0.2–0.5
(3MTQ+1RW) to 0.000 (3MTQ+3RW, full authority), and convergence rises with it.
**Absolute convergence % on the P1.1/P1.2 rows is on the deliberately harsh
allocation testbed and must not be compared to Paper 2's P2.1** (different
framework gains) — cite P1.1 as paired LP1−LP2 deltas and 1−ᾱ, P1.2 as the
*relative* across-config trend. The P1.1 rows are now clustered as adjacent
**LP1 (single-stage LP) / LP2 (two-stage LP)** pairs per (config, h₀ level), so
the paired LP1−LP2 mean-error delta and the 1−ᾱ comparison read off directly:
on 3+1 the single-stage LP1 has the *lower* mean error (e.g. h0: 47.9° vs 55.8°,
Δ −7.9°; h.9: 13.3° vs 16.6°, Δ −3.3°) at a marginally lower 1−ᾱ — i.e. the
two-stage LP2 does not help (and slightly hurts) on this underactuated testbed;
on 3+3 both are identical (fully actuated). Per-row paired Δ is in the digest's
`basis` column. P1.3 is the law-swap
demonstration, now with all four laws framework-allocated (MTQ+RW) on both
vector and full-attitude tasks: on the on-design vector task the four are
comparable (53–70%), and on full attitude LP-PD/Wie lead (44–47%) with the
RW-enabled magnetic laws trailing but converging (22–37%). Diagonal /
controllability-appropriate cells (P1.2) are the portability headline; the
off-diagonal 3MTQ+0RW-on-full-attitude cell is the negative control.
