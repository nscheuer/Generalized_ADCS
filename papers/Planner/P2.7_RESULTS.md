# P2.7 — Planner hyperparameter sensitivity sweep (nice-to-have)

**Goal.** Characterize how the planner's convergence, accuracy and solve time
respond to its main tuning knobs — a robustness check for Paper 2.

## Environment
- Python 3.12.13, NumPy 2.2.6, SciPy 1.15.3; Patricks-MacBook-Pro.local
  (Darwin 25.4.0, arm64). Branch `paper2-datagen`, commit `33aa11d` + this
  script. SALTRO `fd7d24c`.

## Method
- Config **3+1**, reduced (vector-pointing) MC, `Plan_and_Track_LQR`.
- **One-at-a-time** sweep around the canonical tuning (baseline shared),
  6 trials/setting, paired seeds, tf = 1000 s:
  - `pass1.aug_lag.penalty_init` ∈ {1e-4, **1e-3**, 1e-2}
  - `cost_main.angle` (attitude weight) ∈ {1, **10**, 100}
  - `cost_main.control_mult` (effort weight) ∈ {0.5, **1.0**, 2.0}
- Per setting: convergence % (final error < 5°, Paper-2 metric), mean final
  pointing error, and one representative plan's solve time.

## Results (6 trials/setting)

| Dimension | Value | Conv % | mean final (deg) | solve (s) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| baseline | — | 100 | 0.139 | 6.5 |
| penalty_init | 1e-4 | 100 | 0.144 | 7.3 |
| penalty_init | 1e-2 | 100 | 0.141 | 7.4 |
| angle | 1 | 100 | 0.103 | **2.2** |
| angle | 100 | 100 | 0.056 | **17.5** |
| control_mult | 0.5 | 100 | 0.078 | 6.8 |
| control_mult | 2.0 | 100 | 0.085 | 5.5 |

Outputs: `output_data/P2.7_sensitivity_<ts>.json`,
`output_data/fig_sensitivity.{png,pdf}` (mean error and solve time vs each knob).

## Reading guide
- **The planner is robust.** Convergence is **100% at every tested setting** and
  the mean final pointing error stays **sub-0.15°** throughout — the published
  tuning is not brittle; none of these knobs breaks convergence over the tested
  range.
- **The dominant sensitivity is solve time vs the attitude cost weight**
  (`cost_main.angle`): solve time spans **2.2 s → 17.5 s** (~8×) from angle=1 to
  100, while accuracy improves only modestly (0.10° → 0.056°). A larger attitude
  weight buys marginally tighter pointing at a steep compute cost — the natural
  knob for a compute-constrained deployment (cf. P2.5 RPi-4 timing) is to *lower*
  `angle`.
- **`penalty_init` is nearly inert** here: ±2 decades changes neither
  convergence nor accuracy and barely moves solve time (6.5–7.4 s). The AL
  penalty schedule is well within its insensitive regime for this task.
- **`control_mult` has a mild, non-monotone effect** on accuracy (best at the
  extremes, 0.078°/0.085°, vs 0.139° at baseline) with flat solve time — within
  noise at n=6; not a strong lever.
- Small sweep by design (nice-to-have). A finer grid or more trials/setting
  would tighten the `control_mult` trend; the headline (robust convergence,
  solve-time/accuracy trade on `angle`) is already clear.
