
Citation Reference
==================

This page lists source literature for control laws and estimators implemented
in the package, linking each algorithm to its original paper.

Control Laws
------------

Lovera Magnetic PD Control
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
*Module:* :class:ADCS.controller.mtq_lovera

M. Lovera and A. Astolfi, "Global Magnetic Attitude Control of Inertially
Pointing Spacecraft," <https://doi.org/10.2514/1.11844>_
*Journal of Guidance, Control, and Dynamics*, Vol. 28, No. 5, 2005, pp. 1065-1072.

Wisniewski Sliding Mode Control
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
*Module:* :class:ADCS.controller.mtq_wisniewski

R. Wisniewski, "Sliding Mode Attitude Control for Magnetic Actuated Satellite,"
<https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-6670(17)41076-7>_
IFAC Proceedings Volumes, Vol. 31, No. 21, 1998, pp. 179-184.

Hogan-Schaub Continuous Momentum Dumping
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
*Module:* :class:ADCS.controller.mtq_w_rw

E. A. Hogan and H. Schaub, "Three-Axis Attitude Control Using Redundant
Reaction Wheels with Continuous Momentum Dumping,"
<https://doi.org/10.2514/1.G000812>_
*Journal of Guidance, Control, and Dynamics*, Vol. 38, No. 10, 2015, pp. 1865-1871.

McKeen-Scheuer-Cahoy Mixed RW-MTQ Allocation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
*Module:* :class:ADCS.controller.mtq_w_rw_LP, :class:ADCS.controller.mtq_w_rw_QP

P. McKeen, N. Scheuer, and K. Cahoy, "Generalized Attitude Control for
Small Spacecraft," <https://digitalcommons.usu.edu/smallsat/2026/all2026/253/>_
40th Annual Small Satellite Conference, SSC26-P2-54, 2026.
