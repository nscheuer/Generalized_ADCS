Forces on a Spacecraft
======================

Orbital forces include Earth’s nonspherical gravity, atmospheric drag, and solar radiation pressure. These examples explore gravity harmonics and SGP4, which represents drag through its aggregate B* term; they do not model a separate solar radiation pressure force on the orbit.
