# EPIC comparison

DSCOVR/EPIC photographs vs the viewer rendered from DSCOVR's position at the image time. Generated by `tools/viz_validation/run_epic_check.py`.

Note on time: EPIC publishes two timestamps per image (`date` and the identifier, ~288 s apart; the natural-colour image is a composite of bands exposed over minutes). The surface pattern and EPIC's centroid both place the image a few minutes after `date`; our Earth orientation itself agrees with JPL Horizons to < 1 arcsec (testing/test_viz/test_vizlog_environment.py).
