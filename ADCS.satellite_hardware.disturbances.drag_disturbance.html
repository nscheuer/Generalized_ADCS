ADCS.satellite_hardware.disturbances.drag_disturbance module¶

class ADCS.satellite_hardware.disturbances.drag_disturbance.Drag_Disturbance(config)[source]¶

Bases: Disturbance

Aerodynamic Drag Disturbance Model

This class models the aerodynamic drag torque acting on a satellite in low Earth orbit (LEO). The drag force on each exposed surface is computed from the relative atmospheric flow in the body frame, and the net disturbance torque is obtained as the cross product of surface forces with their lever arms from the satellite’s center of mass.

Physical Model

The drag force acting on a satellite surface is modeled as:

\[\mathbf{F}_i = -\tfrac{1}{2} \rho \, C_{D,i} \, A_i \, \max(0, \mathbf{n}_i \cdot \mathbf{V}_b) \, \frac{\mathbf{V}_b}{\|\mathbf{V}_b\|}\]

where:

  • \(\rho\) — Atmospheric density [kg/m³]

  • \(C_{D,i}\) — Drag coefficient of the i-th surface

  • \(A_i\) — Area of the i-th surface [m²]

  • \(\mathbf{n}_i\) — Surface normal (unit vector) in the body frame

  • \(\mathbf{V}_b\) — Relative velocity vector in the body frame [m/s]

The total drag torque acting on the spacecraft is:

\[\mathbf{T}_{\mathrm{drag}} = \sum_i (\mathbf{r}_i - \mathbf{r}_{\mathrm{COM}}) \times \mathbf{F}_i\]

where \(\mathbf{r}_i\) is the centroid of the i-th surface and \(\mathbf{r}_{\mathrm{COM}}\) is the satellite center of mass.

Parameters:

config (GeometryConfig) – Configuration object containing face areas, centroids, normals, and drag coefficients for each surface element.

torque(sat, x, os)[source]¶

Compute the aerodynamic drag torque in the body frame.

Using the force model above, the implemented torque expression is

\[\mathbf{T}_{\mathrm{drag}} = -\tfrac{1}{2}\,\rho \sum_i C_{D,i}A_i \max\!\big(0,\ \mathbf{n}_i^\top\mathbf{V}_b\big) \big(\mathbf{r}_i-\mathbf{r}_{\mathrm{COM}}\big)\times \mathbf{V}_b.\]

State dependency. The full state \(x\) contains the attitude quaternion \(\mathbf{q}\); the relative flow \(\mathbf{V}_b=\mathbf{V}_b(\mathbf{q})\) is provided by Orbital_State.

Parameters:

  • sat (Satellite) – Satellite instance providing the COM position via sat.COM.

  • x (numpy.ndarray) – Full spacecraft state; must contain the attitude quaternion.

  • os (Orbital_State) – Provides body-frame relative velocity \(\mathbf{V}_b\) and density \(\rho\).

Returns:

Total aerodynamic drag torque [N·m], shape (3,).

Return type:

numpy.ndarray

torque_qjac(sat, x, os)[source]¶

Compute the Jacobian of drag torque with respect to the attitude quaternion.

Let \(\mathbf{q}\) be the attitude quaternion inside \(x\). The torque depends on \(\mathbf{q}\) via \(\mathbf{V}_b(\mathbf{q})\) and via the term \(\max(0,\,\mathbf{n}_i^\top\mathbf{V}_b)\). Using \(H(\cdot)\) for the Heaviside function,

\[\frac{\partial}{\partial \mathbf{q}} \max\!\big(0,\ \mathbf{n}_i^\top\mathbf{V}_b\big) = H\!\big(\mathbf{n}_i^\top\mathbf{V}_b\big)\, \mathbf{n}_i^\top\frac{\partial \mathbf{V}_b}{\partial \mathbf{q}}.\]

The resulting Jacobian is consistent with the implemented expression

\[\frac{\partial \mathbf{T}_{\mathrm{drag}}}{\partial \mathbf{q}} = -\tfrac{1}{2}\rho\Big[ \big(\tfrac{\partial F}{\partial \mathbf{q}}\cdot\mathbf{r}\big)\times\mathbf{V}_b + \big(F\cdot\mathbf{r}\big)\times \frac{\partial \mathbf{V}_b}{\partial \mathbf{q}} \Big],\]

where \(F_i = C_{D,i}A_i\max(0,\mathbf{n}_i^\top\mathbf{V}_b)\) and \(\mathbf{r}\) stacks the lever arms \((\mathbf{r}_i-\mathbf{r}_{\mathrm{COM}})\).

Parameters:

  • sat (Satellite) – Satellite instance providing the COM.

  • x (numpy.ndarray) – Full spacecraft state containing the attitude quaternion \(\mathbf{q}\).

  • os (Orbital_State) – Provides \(\mathbf{V}_b\), density \(\rho\), and the quaternion derivative \(\partial \mathbf{V}_b / \partial \mathbf{q}\).

Returns:

Quaternion Jacobian ∂T_drag/∂q, shape (3, 4).

Return type:

numpy.ndarray

torque_qqhess(sat, x, os)[source]¶

Compute the Hessian of drag torque with respect to the attitude quaternion.

With \(\mathbf{q}\) the attitude quaternion, define \(F_i(\mathbf{q}) = C_{D,i}A_i\max(0,\mathbf{n}_i^\top\mathbf{V}_b(\mathbf{q}))\). Then

\[\frac{\partial^2 \mathbf{T}_{\mathrm{drag}}}{\partial \mathbf{q}^2} = -\tfrac{1}{2}\rho\Big[ \frac{\partial^2 (F\cdot\mathbf{r})}{\partial \mathbf{q}^2}\times\mathbf{V}_b + \frac{\partial (F\cdot\mathbf{r})}{\partial \mathbf{q}}\times \frac{\partial \mathbf{V}_b}{\partial \mathbf{q}} + \big(\cdot\big)^\top + (F\cdot\mathbf{r})\times \frac{\partial^2 \mathbf{V}_b}{\partial \mathbf{q}^2} \Big],\]

where \((\cdot)^\top\) denotes the term with the cross-product factors swapped, matching the implemented symmetric pairing.

Parameters:

  • sat (Satellite) – Satellite instance providing the COM.

  • x (numpy.ndarray) – Full spacecraft state containing the attitude quaternion \(\mathbf{q}\).

  • os (Orbital_State) – Provides \(\mathbf{V}_b\), \(\rho\), and derivatives \(\partial \mathbf{V}_b / \partial \mathbf{q}\), \(\partial^2 \mathbf{V}_b / \partial \mathbf{q}^2\).

Returns:

Quaternion Hessian tensor ∂²T_drag/∂q², shape (3, 4, 4).

Return type:

numpy.ndarray
